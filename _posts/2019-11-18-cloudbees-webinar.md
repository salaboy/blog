I am super happy to announce that I am doing a Webinar about how to go ***"From Monoliths to Kubernetes using Jenkins X"***. This is being organised by CloudBees and it is going to happen the [26th Nov 2019](https://register.gotowebinar.com/register/4496029892230348035). I will be showing live how to work with multiple projects and what main challenges are you going to face while going cloud native, along the tools ([Zeebe](http://zeebe.io), Helm, etc.) that will help you to move faster.

[![Webinar](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/salaboy/blog-assets@v1/images/2019/11/webinar.jpg)](https://register.gotowebinar.com/register/4496029892230348035)![From monolith to k8s](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/salaboy/blog-assets@v1/images/2019/11/from-monolith-to-k8s.png)

If you are interested in the webinar please register here:  <https://register.gotowebinar.com/register/4496029892230348035>

I'm really looking forward to hear your questions and comments about it.
