To all the students out there: check the Google Summer of Code program ideas this year related to Drools & jBPM: <https://developer.jboss.org/wiki/JBossCommunityGoogleSummerOfCode2016Ideas>

![GSoC2016Logo](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/salaboy/blog-assets@v1/images/2016/02/gsoc2016logo.jpg)

I'm looking forward to participate for the 4th year in a row as Mentor, so get in touch if you want to participate of this year program. Hopefully this year JBoss will get accepted again as an open source organisation. So we will be looking for students to implement this proposals.

There are 4 proposed ideas under Application Development:

- - [Drools IoT (Drools + Pi4J) - Drools Demo into the Raspberry PI](https://developer.jboss.org/wiki/JBossCommunityGoogleSummerOfCode2016Ideas#jive_content_id_Drools_IoT_Drools__Pi4J__Drools_Demo_into_the_Raspberry_PI)
  - [Drools Rules in Minecraft](https://developer.jboss.org/wiki/JBossCommunityGoogleSummerOfCode2016Ideas#jive_content_id_Drools_Rules_in_Minecraft)
  - [Integrate Drools with your favourite project](https://developer.jboss.org/wiki/JBossCommunityGoogleSummerOfCode2016Ideas#jive_content_id_Integrate_Drools_with_your_favourite_project)
  - [KIE Management Application (with REST api and UI)](https://developer.jboss.org/wiki/JBossCommunityGoogleSummerOfCode2016Ideas#jive_content_id_KIE_Management_Application_with_REST_api_and_UI)

And one under Management &  Monitoring:

- [Improve existing Drools Monitoring Capabilities](https://developer.jboss.org/wiki/JBossCommunityGoogleSummerOfCode2016Ideas#jive_content_id_Improve_existing_Drools_Monitoring_Capabilities)

Let us know what you think about them, and we might work together on getting this done during Summer Time.

Stay tuned for more information on the up coming weeks.
