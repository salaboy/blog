Hi everyone, we @ Plugtree are planning to do an open training in London, UK during the second week of August (on the 8th and 9th).

We usually do private and on-site training, but for the first time, we would like to do a training session mixing attendees with different backgrounds, so we all can learn from each other´s experiences. We are planning two sessions: the first day is focused completely on Drools Expert and the second on jBPM5.  Don’t worry, we will cover Drools Fusion by using examples that use the CEP features.  No previous experience with these projects is needed, but Java coding will be required. We will be prepared to assist you in case that you get lost in some exercise.

According to the participants who  register, each topic will be customized to cover exactly what you are looking for. We are used to doing workshop-like sessions to guide people through the different features and most common patterns.  Because we are also traveling from abroad, we can choose a good hotel to stay with all the attendees and also have coding nights :)

If you are interested, please post a comment or please emails us to: info AT plugtree DOT com or my personal email: salaboy AT gmail DOT com, using the subject: Drools and jBPM5 Training in London.

[Here is the eventbrite event page to register yourself](http://www.eventbrite.com/event/1934320605).  Esteban ([@ilestbean](http://www.twitter.com/ilesteban), [blog](http://ilesteban.wordpress.com/2011/07/18/drools-and-jbpm5-open-training-london-august-8th9th/)) and I([@salaboy](http://www.twitter.com/salaboy)) will be blogging and tweeting about this community training effort. Stay tuned for more information but please let us know if you are interested.
