I had an amazing time in San Francisco, the Jenkins community is amazing. It was a big milestone for Jenkins, the project is thriving after 15 years of its creation.

![The jenkins](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/salaboy/blog-assets@v1/images/2019/08/the-jenkins.png)![#JenkinsWorld 2019, San Fransisco, USA](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/salaboy/blog-assets@v1/images/2019/08/img_3042.png)![Salaboy at jenkins world](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/salaboy/blog-assets@v1/images/2019/08/salaboy-at-jenkins-world-1.png)![15 years 2](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/salaboy/blog-assets@v1/images/2019/08/15-years-2-1.png)![15 years](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/salaboy/blog-assets@v1/images/2019/08/15-years-1.png)![Keynote mention](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/salaboy/blog-assets@v1/images/2019/08/keynote-mention-1.png)

The most amazing thing with these events is the fact that you manage to put names to faces and see how people collaborate together to get stuff done, no matter the background or even companies interest.

My presentation was all about Building Cloud Native Platforms with the help of Jenkins X and all the challenges that you will face in a real life project.

[You can find more about my presentation and all the links for the demo that I showed here.](/2019/08/12/building-cloud-native-platforms-with-jenkins-x/)

I'm really happy to see how the [CDFoundation](http://cd.foundation) is shaping up and I do expect a lot of more things happening this year on that front. The CD Foundation really matters and I hope that the entire Kubernetes community helps to support this initiative.
