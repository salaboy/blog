I am completely blown away by the [Spring One Platform](http://springoneplatform.io) conference. Amazing sessions packed with roadmaps and demos that I was not expecting to see. I hope to be able to join next year with more cool stuff about Spring Cloud Kubernetes, KNative, Istio and Activiti Cloud.

Some of the highlights were:

- Spring Cloud Functions
- Riff on top of KNative
- [R2DBC](https://github.com/r2dbc)
- [RSocket](http://rsocket.io)
