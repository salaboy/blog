A few weeks ago, I had a fantastic time at KCD UK 2024. KCDs are great events to have a lot of interesting conversations and meet friends from London and abroad.

I had the pleasure of presenting with [Abby](https://x.com/a_bangser) from [Syntasso](https://www.syntasso.io/) about the intersection between platform and development teams. We focused on three key challenges development teams face when working with complex infrastructure. If you are interested in this topic, check my blog post here: <https://www.diagrid.io/blog/when-platform-teams-meet-developers>

I cover tools like [vcluster](https://www.vcluster.com/), [DevPod](https://devpod.sh/), [DevBox](https://www.jetify.com/devbox), [Dagger](https://dagger.io/), [Dapr](https://dapr.io/), [OpenFeature](https://openfeature.dev/) to alleviate some of these challenges, but we made a great point about making sure that platform teams choose the right tool to enable their teams based on what they are doing.
