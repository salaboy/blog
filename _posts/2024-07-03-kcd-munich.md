I had an amazing time at [KCD Munich](https://www.kcdmunich.de/) this week. KCDs are always fun because you get to see where the communities are, which topics are hot and you meet a lot of project maintainers. Kudos to the organizers who did an amazing job at having an amazing third edition of the conference.

This time, I had the pleasure of presenting with [Thomas Vitale](https://x.com/vitalethomas) about platform engineering and how we can unlock new experiences by relying on open interfaces.

![KCD Munich - Platforms, Cloud Native and Communities](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/salaboy/blog-assets@v1/images/2024/07/IMG_1889.JPG)

I also had the chance to do a book signing session. Thanks to [Loft Labs](https://www.loft.sh/) for sponsoring the copies!

![Screenshot 2024 07 03 at 11.50.](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/salaboy/blog-assets@v1/images/2024/07/Screenshot-2024-07-03-at-11.50.05.png)![Thanks for the support Abdel and Christian](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/salaboy/blog-assets@v1/images/2024/07/IMG_1885.JPG)![KCD Munich - Platforms, Cloud Native and Communities](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/salaboy/blog-assets@v1/images/2024/07/IMG_1883.JPG)

*Thanks for the support Abdel and Christian*

We had the chance to do an unconference session around the [CNCF App Delivery TAG and the Working Groups](https://tag-app-delivery.cncf.io/wgs/) under the TAG. Thanks to [Lian](https://x.com/lianmakesthings) for organizing and leading it.

![KCD Munich - Platforms, Cloud Native and Communities](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/salaboy/blog-assets@v1/images/2024/07/IMG_1848-1.jpeg)

Big Kudos to [Henrik Rexed](https://x.com/Hrexed) from Dynatrace for always recording high-quality videos and interviews.

I took three main takeaways and action items:

- Add Dapr to [CNOE](https://cnoe.io/), it shouldn't be that difficult. Ask about Knative, too.
- Try out [mirrord by Metalbear](https://github.com/metalbear-co/mirrord) with sidecars.
- Make sure that I submit it again next year!

I will add the recording to this blog post when it is out! Now it is time to go back to London and do some coding!
