I am preparing myself for a crazy week at KubeCon EU next week. There are tons of things going on, and I am excited to meet everyone coming to London. It is interesting to have a KubeCon happening in the city where you live, so let's see how it goes.

My week is going to be hectic, but as usual, the most important factor for me is to meet people, make new connections, and learn about what is going on in the cloud native space.

Before we start all activities on Sunday with Cloud Native Rejekts, I am joining the "Is it Observable?" webinar with my friend Henrik Rexed today, Friday 28th, at 4pm. You can join us here: <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7Pv2ktWr2cw>

[![Screenshot 2025 03 28 at 14.23.](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/salaboy/blog-assets@v1/images/2025/03/Screenshot-2025-03-28-at-14.23.13.png)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7Pv2ktWr2cw )

#### Cloud Native Rejeckts (30th/31st March 25)

Starting with [Cloud Native Rejeckts](https://cloud-native.rejekts.io/)excellent on Sunday, I am looking forward to that pre-conference vibe with extremely good speakers and smaller rooms for deeper conversations.

Highlights on Sunday:

- [Podman and Podman Desktop: State of the Union](https://cfp.cloud-native.rejekts.io/cloud-native-rejekts-europe-london-2025/talk/3RZV83/)
- [Building Air-Gapped Control Planes for a Global Pharma Leader Using Crossplane and ArgoCD](https://cfp.cloud-native.rejekts.io/cloud-native-rejekts-europe-london-2025/talk/P7RZJ7/)

Highlights on Monday:

- [Simplifying cross-cloud, cross-cluster connectivity with Dapr & Cilium](https://cfp.cloud-native.rejekts.io/cloud-native-rejekts-europe-london-2025/talk/DEMXNY/)
- [Making GenAI CLI Agents Work for Your DevOps Day-to-Day Operations](https://cfp.cloud-native.rejekts.io/cloud-native-rejekts-europe-london-2025/talk/MKR7YL/)

Unfortunately, on Monday, I cannot attend the presentation from Alice Gibbons and Manuel Zapf, who will be showing Cillium and Dapr working together across clusters. If you have the chance to participate in the session, I totally recommend it.

#### Maintainers Summit (31st March 25)

On Monday, I am eager to see how the [Maintainers Summit](https://maintainersummiteu2025.sched.com/) goes. While this event is not for everyone, I will take enough notes to share with a broader audience. I am really looking forward to the [TAG restructuring workshop](https://sched.co/1uSN7) as this represents a significant re org of how advisory groups inside the CNCF will work. Specifically, I am keen to see how I can be part of the TAG Developer Experience, which is key to bringing developers closer to the cloud native space.
