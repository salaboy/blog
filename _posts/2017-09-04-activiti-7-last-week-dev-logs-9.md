Last week (**Week 29/8/17 - 3/9/17**) was quiet, most of the team is on holidays, but we manage to make some progress with [@daisuke-yoshimoto](https://github.com/daisuke-yoshimoto) and [@gmalanga](https://github.com/gmalanga), where two new implementations for the query(ElasticSearch) and audit(MongoDB) module are in the way and pull requests are going to be reviewed this week.

On my side ([@salaboy](http://twitter.com/salaboy)) I manage to make some very good progress on the kubernetes deployments descriptors and I got our services working in minikube and a real kubernetes implementation with minimal effort. There will be a blog post about this in the next couple of days.

This week, the focus will be in documenting all the new modules, preparing for release to maven central and making sure that the community can run our cloud examples in less than 10 minutes (excluding download times :)

Stay tuned!
