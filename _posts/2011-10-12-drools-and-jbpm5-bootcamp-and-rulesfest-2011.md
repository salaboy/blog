We are 10 days away from the beginning of Rules Fest 2011, a very unique event where most of the Rule Engine vendors meet up to show the latest research and innovations inside the niche. This will be my third Rules Fest in a row and I will be presenting the [Emergency Service](/2011/05/28/emergency-services-v2-jbpm5-and-drools-blueprint/) application and talking about [jBPM5](http://jbpm.org) there. If you are around San Francisco, USA (OCTOBER 24-27) and if you have interest to learn about Rule Engines I strongly recommend you to attend. You need to pay some bucks in order to attend to the main event, but this year there will be 2 free boot camps (2 free days) about [Drools](http://www.drools.org) and [jBPM5](http://www.jbpm.org). One day will be pretty related with technology and the other will be focused in the health care industry.

I'm getting ready for the event and I will be publishing my presentations and a couple of posts to gather feedback about some ideas that I want to share during the event.

Here are some reference links where you can find more information about the event and the required information to register yourself.
