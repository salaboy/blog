Hi people, yesterday [Kris](http://kverlaen.blogspot.com/2010/05/proposal-for-jbpm5-roadmap.html) give us great news about the future of jBPM 5. You can find more information in his [blog](http://kverlaen.blogspot.com/2010/05/proposal-for-jbpm5-roadmap.html) and in a [wiki](http://community.jboss.org/wiki/jBPM5Roadmap) page at JBoss.

It will be very interesting to participate in this project, because it will unify different perspectives and it will be a great implementation of the BPMN2 specification. It's a really nice opportunity to get involved, so if you want to be part of the project or if you want to help in some way join the mailing list: [jbpm-dev@lists.jboss.org](mailto:jbpm-dev@lists.jboss.org)

Stay tuned for more news!
