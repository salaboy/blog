Girls, Boys don't miss this amazing opportunity of meeting the Drools and jBPM team leaders in New York and Washington D.C.

Mark (Drools Expert), Edson (Drools Fusion) and Kris (jBPM5) will be visiting the Big Apple(NY) [Tuesday, June 19 2012 in New York City](http://app.engage.redhat.com/e/er?s=1795&lid=1384&elq=d3d6d783acbc4c7382b30a46edde5906) and D.C [Thursday, June 21, 2012 (Government focus) in Washington DC](http://app.engage.redhat.com/e/er?s=1795&lid=5482&elq=d3d6d783acbc4c7382b30a46edde5906) . Follow the links and register yourselves right now!

After those workshops you can follow the team to Boston ([JudCon / JBoss World](http://www.jboss.org/events/JUDCon/2012/boston/travel)), because they are gonna have a great time there as well!

I will miss the tour this year, but I'm getting ready for the next one! Feel free to write me if you have some cool stuff that you want to show to the traveling team members so I can get you in touch with them. I will be trying to cover both events remotely and hopefully I will try to send them something to show about my current work :)

Stay tuned!
