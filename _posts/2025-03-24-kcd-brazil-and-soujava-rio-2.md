I had a fantastic time presenting at the SouJava Rio meetup and KCD Brazil, which happened in Rio this year. It is always fun to meet the local communities, visit old friends and make new ones.

I don't have enough words to thank the organizers of [KCD Brazil](https://community.cncf.io/kcd-brasil/) ([Carol](https://www.linkedin.com/in/carolgv/), you are the best!) and my friend [Matheuz Cruz](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mcruzdev1/) from Itau, the folks from [Impulso](https://www.linkedin.com/company/impulsoteam) and the folks from [SouJava](https://soujava.org.br/) who always welcome and promote knowledge sharing in the Java community.

Unfortunately, I did a terrible job taking pictures, so if you took a picture with me, please share it via DM on any social network.

### The Keynote

Doing a keynote is always challenging as it comes with a lot of responsibility. Still, it was an absolute honor to talk about [Platform Engineering on Kubernetes](/book/), the book, and the work happening in the CNCF and the [Dapr](https://dapr.io) project with the Brazilian community.

### The Dapr Backstage Plugin

It was great fun to meet [Francisco Meneses from Red Hat](https://www.linkedin.com/in/rh-pancho/) to do the world premiere of the [Dapr Backstage Plugin](https://github.com/dapr-sandbox/dapr-backstage)! As we mentioned in the presentation, we need you! If you want to learn and contribute to an Open Source project, reach out to us!

### SouJava, Dapr and Spring Boot

I had a great time visiting the [Impulso](https://www.linkedin.com/company/impulsoteam/) team offices in Rio. We had a good chat about the architecture of Dapr, how it integrates with Spring Boot, and how the project lets developers focus on coding instead of learning about ActiveMQ.

Thank you so much for being so welcoming!
