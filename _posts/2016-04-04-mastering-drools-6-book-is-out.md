Hi everyone, just a quick post to share the good news! The book is out and ready to ship! You can buy it from [Packt](https://www.packtpub.com/networking-and-servers/mastering-jboss-drools-6) or from [Amazon](http://www.amazon.co.uk/Mastering-JBoss-Drools-6-Developers/dp/1783288620/ref=sr_1_1_twi_pap_2?ie=UTF8&qid=1459630733&sr=8-1&keywords=mastering+drools+6) directly. I'm happy to announce also that we are going to be presenting the book next week in Denmark, with the local JBug: <http://www.meetup.com/jbug-dk/events/229407454/> if you are around or know someone that might be interested in attending please let them know!

![Mastering Drools 6](https://www.packtpub.com/sites/default/files/1512_8625OS_Mastering%20JBoss%20Drools%206_0.jpg)

*Mastering Drools 6*

The book covers a wide range of topics from the basic ones including how to set up your environment and how to write simple rules, to more advanced topics such as Complex Event Processing and the core of the Rule Engine, the PHREAK algorithm.
