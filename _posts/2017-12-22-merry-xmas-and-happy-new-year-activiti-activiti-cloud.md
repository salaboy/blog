After having an amazing second half of the year with the Activiti Team I'm going away in holidays for 3 weeks. I'm excited about the plans for next year, and knowing that the December release is on the way closes the year quite perfectly.

Next year will be all about getting the first release of the new Activiti & Activiti Cloud projects. There are a lot of challenges yet to be solved but we will deal with them following the Open Source way. The Activiti Community, Partners and companies are already benefiting from our monthly releases and next year our commitment to them will go one step further.

I'm about to board my flight, so Merry Xmas and Happy New Year to you all!

See you all at DevCon or as usual in our gitter channel. For weekly devlogs please keep an eye on the Alfresco BPM space in jive where the activiti team will write about the December release and the progress made during the next 3 weeks.
