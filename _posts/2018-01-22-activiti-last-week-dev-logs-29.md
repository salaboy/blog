Last week the team presented Activiti Cloud at [DevCon](https://devcon.alfresco.com/) in Lisbon. We missed [@erdemedeiros](https://github.com/erdemedeiros) but we met @igdianov  and @daisuke-yoshimoto at DevCon so we manage to do a face-to-face planning sessions and brainstorming about the future of the projects. This week we will be doing heavy planning for our up coming monthly release plus we will be closing up some of the open topics that we had from the last previous months. We are making great progress on our kubernetes deployments, so we are looking forward to get some feedback from the community. At DevCon we introduced our refreshingly new Logo for the Activiti & Activiti Cloud projects and we are working on a new website to host the new projects:

<https://activiti.github.io>

![Activiti &amp;amp; Activiti Cloud](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/salaboy/blog-assets@v1/images/2018/01/acitiviti_icon_fullcolor_github_400x400.png "Activiti &amp;amp; Activiti Cloud")

*Activiti & Activiti Cloud*

[@constantin-ciobotaru](https://github.com/constantin-ciobotaru) added back contracts consumer tests and investigated ConfigMaps in Kubernetes

[@mteodori](https://github.com/mteodori) attended to [DevCon](https://devcon.alfresco.com/) with the Activiti Team.

[@lucianoprea](https://github.com/lucianoprea) investigated a easy way to turn on debug logging in our apps [#1678](https://github.com/Activiti/Activiti/issues/1678) and looked into an activiti cloud connector developed in another language [#1672](https://github.com/Activiti/Activiti/issues/1672)

[@ffazzini](http://github.com/ffazzini) finished to integrate activiti in alfresco-dbp anaxes, fixed swagger in audit service

[@ryandawsonuk](https://github.com/ryandawsonuk) attended DevCon and presented the trending topics Twitter example in the Activiti Cloud Deep Dive session

[@erdemedeiros](https://github.com/erdemedeiros) worked on PoCs for changes on connectors integration mechanism (<https://github.com/Activiti/Activiti/issues/1680>, <https://github.com/Activiti/Activiti/issues/1681> and <https://github.com/Activiti/Activiti/issues/1666>). Integrated [Awaitility](https://github.com/awaitility/awaitility) library to handle asynchronous tests (<https://github.com/Activiti/Activiti/issues/1558>).

[@salaboy](http://twitter.com/salaboy) presented at DevCon an introduction to the main concepts in Activiti Cloud.
