Next week (Feb 10th to 14th 2025), I have the pleasure of visiting Tokyo, Japan, to talk about the past, present, and future of platform engineering in the context of Kubernetes and the cloud native ecosystem.

I was invited to present the Japanese-translated version of my book [Platform Engineering on Kubernetes](/book) at the [Developers Summit 2025 conference](https://event.shoeisha.jp/devsumi).

![PFEoK syoei](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/salaboy/blog-assets@v1/images/2025/02/PFEoK-syoei.jpg)

Check my session here: <https://event.shoeisha.jp/devsumi/20250213/session/5546>

[![Screenshot 2025 02 03 at 09.23.](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/salaboy/blog-assets@v1/images/2025/02/Screenshot-2025-02-03-at-09.23.12.png)](https://event.shoeisha.jp/devsumi)

I am thrilled to go, as I will finally meet [@nwiizo](https://x.com/nwiizo), who is an absolute legend, as he translated all the tutorials in the book repository to Japanese and participated in the translation of the book itself. If you are not following him, you should!

I will be signing book copies with [@nwiizo](https://x.com/nwiizo) here, too, so if you are around Tokyo, come and say hello!

[![Japanese book](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/salaboy/blog-assets@v1/images/2025/02/japanese-book.jpeg)](https://x.com/devsumi/status/1885208436272750739)

If this were not enough, the [Platform Engineering meetup](https://platformengineering.connpass.com/event/342670/) group in Tokyo organized a fantastic event where I will also be presenting with other community speakers:

[![Screenshot 2025 02 03 at 09.32.](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/salaboy/blog-assets@v1/images/2025/02/Screenshot-2025-02-03-at-09.32.05.png)](https://platformengineering.connpass.com/event/342670/)

I must share my appreciation to [@jacopen](https://x.com/jacopen), [@nwiizo](https://x.com/nwiizo) and all the organizers and speakers for setting up this fantastic event. Click on the image to reserve your spot (in-person or online), as seats are running out.

[![Jjug2](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/salaboy/blog-assets@v1/images/2025/02/jjug2.png)](https://x.com/JJUG)

I will also present at the [JJUG (Japanese Java User Group)](https://jjug.doorkeeper.jp/events/181578) about the evolution of cloud-native and Java, focusing on how Dapr complements tools like Spring Boot and Quarkus. Please register asap, as space is running out too.

[![Screenshot 2025 02 03 at 09.49.](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/salaboy/blog-assets@v1/images/2025/02/Screenshot-2025-02-03-at-09.49.17.png)](https://jjug.doorkeeper.jp/events/181578)

I am looking forward to meet with my friends [Kohei Ota](https://x.com/_inductor_) from Apple and [Shinya Yanagihara](https://x.com/yanashin18618) from Microsoft who are always supportive and helping me to connect with different communities.

I am looking forward to meeting the Japanese communities; if you want to talk about Platform Engineering, Dapr, or the cloud native ecosystem and you are around Tokyo, come and say hello. I am always happy to have a chat and meet new folks in the ever-changing cloud native space.

### After meeting the Japanese community

Last week, I had a fantastic time in Tokyo, Japan, meeting with the [CNIA PFEM](https://x.com/cnia_pfem) community and their [Java User Group](https://x.com/JJUG), who hosted a meetup at the Microsoft offices.

Thanks so much, [@jacopen](https://x.com/jacopen) and the Cloud Native Innovators Alliance, for organizing a fantastic event with a huge turnout. I was mind-blown 🤯.

I also had the pleasure of hanging out with the folks from [AP Communications](https://www.ap-com.co.jp/service/) who are deep into building platforms. I am looking forward to collaborating more with you all. Big shoutout to [Hitoshi](https://x.com/turtle2005) and

Big shoutout to [Shinya from Microsoft](https://x.com/yanashin18618) and [Kaito from HPE](https://x.com/kaitoii11) for all your support and all the effort that you folks put into building relationships across communities.
