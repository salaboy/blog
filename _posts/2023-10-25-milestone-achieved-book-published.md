I am happy to announce that my book [Platform Engineering on Kubernetes](/book/) is officially out! You can get the final version of the first edition via the Manning website. You can also order a printed version!

![Book cover](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/salaboy/blog-assets@v1/images/2023/10/book-cover-1.jpg)

Remember that you can access, use, and share all step-by-step tutorials hosted at <https://github.com/salaboy/platforms-on-k8s> . If you like the tutorials, please give it a start ⭐️ so the repository gets recommended to people interested in these topics. If you want to contribute to improving or extending these examples, please reach out. I would love to collaborate!

This book would be impossible to write without the support and experience of so many good people from different communities and backgrounds. I did collect a lot of notes about the writing experience, how I make it work for me, and why **you should never write a book about Cloud Native technologies** 😉 , just kidding!

I will share those learnings as blog posts, or I can even submit a talk to a conference if people find the topic interesting.

Thanks to all who reached out via Twitter or Linkedin.

### **What’s next?**

The second edition already sounds like a possibility, as I left a lot of content out, and I couldn’t cover all the topics I wanted, primarily because of a lack of time and the fact that the project was already taking too long. I’ve got a lot of feedback from the MEAP program (early access), so I have a good idea of which topics must be included in the next iteration.

But much closer than a second edition, I am attending [KubeCon NA Chicago](https://events.linuxfoundation.org/kubecon-cloudnativecon-north-america/) in a couple of weeks and hoping to meet you all there.

I am super excited that Diagrid, the company where I am working, decided to buy some copies to share with the attendees so that I will be doing some book signing (Wed Nov 8th between 12:30-2:00pm) at the Diagrid sponsor booth (B27) during the conference. Check this blog post for more details about this: <https://www.diagrid.io/blog/diagrid-dapr-kubecon-north-america-2023>

If you are attending co-located events, I will present at the first edition of [AppDeveloperCon](https://events.linuxfoundation.org/kubecon-cloudnativecon-north-america/co-located-events/appdevelopercon/) with my friend Oleg from [AtomicJar](https://www.atomicjar.com/). We will also have some books to share at AppDeveloperCon (Diagrid sponsor booth (table F6)).

I will also be popping up to Codefresh’s booth as they will share with attendees some copies of the [Software Deployment with Kubernetes report](https://codefresh.io/ebooks/software-deployment-with-kubernetes/) that I authored based on the book’s content. You can check what Codefresh is planning for the event on their website: <https://codefresh.io/kubecon/>

If you are going, reach out. My DMs on Twitter [@Salaboy](https://twitter.com/salaboy) are open, and I am actively looking for party recommendations!
