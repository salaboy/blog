Welcome back, here I'm posting the second module of my [jBPM5 community training course](/2011/01/24/announcing-jbpm5-community-training/). This module is about the Business Process Management practice. This module covers the fundamentals and must known aspects that you must understand to start using jBPM5 with the right foot. As you may know, this is the first revision of the content, so feedback is really appreciated!

![Jbpm5 fix1](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/salaboy/blog-assets@v1/images/2011/02/jbpm5-fix1.png "jBPM5")

## Module #2: The BPM Practice

The following topics will be covered inside this module:

- What is a Business Process? Why do we need them?
- Advantages of knowing your business processes
- Business Process Management Introduction
  - Stage #1: Discover
  - Stage #2: Formalize
  - Stage #3: Implement
  - Stage #4: Runtime
  - Stage #5: Monitor
  - Stage #6: Improve
- Conclusions
