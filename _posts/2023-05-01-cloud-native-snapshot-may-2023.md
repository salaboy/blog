First of all, I want to thank you all for your support. Creating these reports will take time and effort, but it is worth the investment.

I will use the first three reports to gather feedback from decision-makers and tech leads who are building platforms on top of Kubernetes and don't have enough time to check every project that comes with the shiny new thing in the Cloud-Native space.

I would appreciate it if you could share this report with your closest decision-makers, CTOs, and architects that can provide feedback. Try not to share this publicly because, at the end of the day, this is for supporters only.

You can download the first report here:

**[Salaboy's Cloud Native Snapshot May 23 (12 MB)](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/salaboy/blog-assets@v1/files/2023/05/Salaboy-Cloud-Native-Snapshot-May-23.pdf)**

This month, I've decided to start with a hot topic in the Cloud-Native space that is still evolving: **Provisioning Cloud Resources**. I started by covering a project and a company I know well to determine the proper format for these reports.

To figure out if these reports are helpful, you can help me out by answering these questions:

- Do you think that the report has enough useful information? What is missing?
- Is the topic relevant for decision-makers and tech leads?
- Is the report length OK? Too long? Too short?
- What would you change?
- What did you like the most?

I will invite all the members (email used for membership) for a 45 mins call to discuss these questions and other topics you might be interested in. I appreciate the support, and I think that by doing live calls, we can share information, keep up to date and discuss other complex topics to cover using different mediums. Would you prefer a chat or email?

If you have any questions, suggestions, or corrections, please reach out. You know where to find me.

Enjoy!
