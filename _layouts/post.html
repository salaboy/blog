--- layout: default --- {%- comment -%} Overrides minima 2.5.2's _layouts/post.html. Identical to the gem's version except for the post-cta include before the closing tag. Kept as a layout rather than 542 edited files so the wording can change in one place and new posts pick it up automatically. The CTA sits outside `.post-content` on purpose: that element carries the `e-content` microformat, so keeping the CTA out of it leaves the machine readable article body as the author's words only. It is likewise absent from the RSS feed, which renders `post.content` and never runs this layout. {%- endcomment -%}
